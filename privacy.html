What we collect

When an invited tester sends a direct message to our Instagram account, Instagram delivers that message to us: the sender's Instagram-scoped ID, the message, and any shared reel or post link with its media. We use this only to test whether shared reels can be saved and organised for the person who sent them.

How we store and use it

Messages and media are stored on a single computer controlled by Tenet Mediaverse, not on public servers. We do not sell, share or publish this data, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to train AI models. Test data is deleted when the test ends.

Your choices and deletion

You can ask us to delete everything we hold about you at any time by emailing tenetmediaversepvtltd@gmail.com or by sending a direct message to our Instagram account. We will delete it within 7 days and confirm.
